# Escrow red-team review, 24 October 2026

## Scope

This review covers the EVM escrow (`contracts/evm/src/ProjectEscrow.sol`) and
the Solana program (`contracts/solana/programs/slop-escrow/src/lib.rs`) in the
open stack #1081 → #1086 → #1091 → #1098 → #1100. It also covers the off-chain
code that builds escrow transactions or decides that a vault is trusted:
both `adapter.ts` files, `contracts/solana/funding.ts`, the owner and Config
planners, and `workers/payment-executor/solana.ts`.

Two independent AI reviewers attacked the #1081 source and the #1091 source.
They proved each finding with a Foundry test or with a transaction on a local
`solana-test-validator`. A third pass reviewed the upgrade code that #1100 adds.
This is an AI review. It is not an independent audit; the owner waived the
audit (PRD section 11).

## Accepted owner policy

These results follow from owner decisions (PRD sections 6, 9, 11 and 18, #1086
and #1100). On 24 October 2026 the owner explicitly accepted the reported
attester-compromise risk and retained immediate binding
([decision](https://github.com/SlopDotCash/slopdotcash/issues/904#issuecomment-6381602244)),
and scoped this review to contract defects: a result that needs a stolen key is
out of scope ("if the drain only happens if a key is stolen, then that is
irrelevant"). Every operator must still know them.

1. **A stolen identity-authority key can take every matured unpaid award.**
   A binding activates immediately and #1100 sets the award delay to zero. The
   holder of the identity key binds a wallet that it controls for any actor and
   calls `pay` in the same transaction (EVM: one EIP-7702 batch or two
   transactions in one block; Solana: one transaction). Both reviewers proved
   this on both chains. With the monthly commitment on, the key can also sign
   an allocation for an actor it controls, up to the monthly cap per period.
2. **The upgrade authority controls all escrowed funds.** #1100 makes the EVM
   factory and every vault UUPS proxies and keeps the Solana program
   upgradeable, with no delay.
3. **Identity-authority rotation has no delay.**

Earlier text such as "the owner can cancel in that time" describes the earlier
delayed design and does not apply to the #1100 deployment.

## Findings fixed on this branch

### F-1 (Medium, Solana): a monthly award's reserve could be locked or refunded

`commit_monthly` reserves the whole month, but awards are committed one leaf at
a time, and the `origin` PDA that makes each source commit once is shared with
the owner's direct `commit`. After admission the owner could commit a leaf's
source by hand first; the leaf could then never commit and nothing released its
reserve. The reviewer proved this on a local validator (20 of 50 USDC-units
reserved for nobody). Three rounds of independent review of the fix produced
the final design:

- `Project.monthly_outstanding` counts admitted gross that has not settled.
  While it is positive, the owner's direct ordinary `commit` fails with
  `MonthlyOutstanding`. Anyone can commit the remaining awards.
- `expire_month(period)`, permissionless from 30 days after the window closes,
  removes a month's unsettled gross from the count once, so an allocation that
  can never settle cannot block the owner for ever. That gross stays reserved.
- The attester signs `frozen_at` with the root (message prefix v3). It must lie
  between the window opening and admission.
- `release_monthly_award` returns a leaf to free balance only when its actor is
  zero, when the source's recorded obligation was committed by hand before
  `frozen_at`, or when another month's award to the same actor for at least the
  same gross consumed it (`["monthly-source", project, source]` marker).
- A `["settled", month, source]` PDA, created by both commit and release, lets
  each leaf settle once.
- `MonthlyAllocation` is 109 bytes (`released`, `frozen_at`, `expired`), so a
  92-byte month from the earlier build fails closed. Devnet has none.

Bugs found in earlier versions of this fix and closed: a committed leaf could
also be released (freeing another leaf's reserve); a legacy month could be
released after migration; a running-maximum gate never lapsed; a source in two
months locked one leaf or refunded a gross difference; and a source consumed
between freeze and admission became a refund. The EVM vault commits a month
atomically and is not affected; #1104's EVM change (count an identical existing
award once) was reviewed and is sound.

### F-2 (Low, Solana; open item L-2 of the 12 October review): the fee leg could stop payouts

`pay` and `withdraw` sent the fee to a fee-recipient token account inside the
same instruction, and the executor and funding flow always used the associated
account. A mint-authority freeze of that account stopped every payout with a
fee. Fees now accrue in the vault (`Project.accrued_fees`), as on EVM; the
permissionless `claim_fees` moves them to any fee-recipient token account.
Vault balance checks hold `reserved + accrued_fees`. `readVaultTotals` reports
`accruedFees` for Solana.

### F-3 (Migration, Solana)

`Project` grows from 316 to 332 bytes. Devnet already has three 316-byte
projects of program `7E5ZRh5y6Xgg7k3kdS75CMiTE2efoq9PqNWR5fCVDavQ`. The
permissionless `migrate_project` grows such an account and starts both new
counters at zero; `config-plan.mjs migrate` builds the unsigned transactions.

### F-4 (Tooling): the local acceptance runner used the old program ID

The #1100 merge had lost #1098's fix, so the runner loaded the program at
`4hXe…` and failed with `DeclaredProgramIdMismatch`. It reads the IDL again.

## Residual items (no contract change)

- **Owner griefing at its own cost (Low).** Between `frozen_at` and admission,
  or after `expire_month`, the owner can hand-commit an unsettled leaf's source.
  The leaf then stays reserved for good; the owner pays twice and gains
  nothing. Before admission the owner can already turn the whole month off.
- **Attester freeze rule (Low, needs an attester fault).** The attester must
  set `frozen_at` to the block time of the finalized slot at which it checked
  that no included source has an `origin`, and must never repeat a source of
  any admitted month. A commit in the same second as `frozen_at` cannot be
  released. No Solana attester code exists yet; whoever builds it must follow
  this rule.
- **An allocation that can never settle (needs an attester fault).** Its
  remainder stays reserved for good; `expire_month` removes only its effect on
  the owner's direct awards.
- **Rotation execution block (EVM and Solana).** With zero delays the owner
  has no cancel window, so this edge from the #1081 source has no effect.
- **Two monthly caps one second apart.** Period windows are adjacent; the rule
  is one cap per period window.
- **The adapters do not compare the on-chain project ID with the registry
  project.** The reviewed manifest pins the exact vault or project PDA.
- **Solana network domain.** Use separate identity keys per cluster, so a
  monthly signature cannot be replayed across clusters.

## Second pass (24 October 2026)

A second EVM red-team, Solana red-team and cross-chain parity review ran at
development `0069433d`. No Critical or High finding; one Medium parity defect
and three Low items.

- **Fixed (#1175): an existing award for a monthly source.** EVM counts an
  identical existing award once. On Solana, an owner hand commit of the same
  award after the freeze locked the monthly leaf's gross for good. Solana now
  releases a leaf whose source already pays the same actor at least its gross
  and net, the freeze and executor mirror it, a Solana month is funded only
  when its whole total is free, and Solana vault totals fail closed on a short
  vault.
- **Fixed: fee splitting.** The 2% fee rounded down per call, so splitting a
  withdrawal or self-award into pieces under 50 base units avoided it (an
  economic gain on 0 to 2 decimal tokens). The owner chose to round up:
  `ceil(gross / 50)` on both chains. Vault logic and the Solana program deployed
  before this change take `floor(gross / 50)` until they are upgraded, which is
  an owner signing step; until then reconciliation accepts either rounding and
  nothing else. A month frozen, or an award reserved, under the old rounding
  stays valid after the upgrade (EVM `commitMonthly` counts such an award once,
  and the attester accepts a snapshot frozen with floor fees). Remove the floor
  acceptance once every deployment runs the new code.
- **Aligned off-chain: wallet-binding edges.** The EVM vault accepts a binding
  at its expiry second and the identity authority as a destination; the Solana
  program rejects both. The EVM attester never submits a signature with less
  than ten minutes left and now refuses the identity authority, or its own key,
  as a destination.
- **In #1162: 18-decimal monthly awards.** The monthly path limits each award
  to `2^64 - 1` base units on both chains, which is Solana's limit but caps an
  18-decimal EVM token near 18.4 tokens per award; #1162 lifts it for EVM.

## Checked and found safe

Both reviewers listed these attack classes as safe on both chains: signer
and owner checks, account and token-program substitution (Token-2022 is
rejected), PDA seeds and bumps, `init_if_needed` reuse, account revival and
type confusion, checked arithmetic with release overflow checks, fee rounding,
the conservation identity, the Ed25519 instruction check, the Merkle sum tree,
EIP-712 domains with low-s and zero-address checks, reentrancy and exact token
deltas, CREATE2 vault keys, the monthly window calendar, and the deploy
scripts. #1100's proxies disable initializers on implementations, initialize
atomically, check `proxiableUUID`, gate `_authorizeUpgrade` on the factory's
upgrade authority, and require acceptance for an upgrade-authority transfer.
